# ROARSounds — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A small WoW addon that restores built-in race-and-sex roar audio from the client when character roar emotes appear in nearby text.

## Design identity

It uses game-supplied sound assets instead of bundling replacements; the reach of other-player identification depends on available unit context.

Retrospective synthesis from the existing README, source and addon metadata.
